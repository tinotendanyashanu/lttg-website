# Employee Portal Handbook

## Purpose
This handbook explains how employees should use the employee portal during deal execution and closing.

Scope is employee-facing routes only. Admin-only pages are intentionally excluded.

## Employee Portal Routes

- ` /portal/login ` - Employee sign-in page.
- ` /portal ` - Main dashboard and daily starting point.
- ` /portal/case-management ` - Track case progress, delivery status, and follow-ups.
- ` /portal/knowledge-base ` - Internal SOPs, scripts, and process guidance.
- ` /portal/resources ` - Download templates, files, and supporting materials.
- ` /portal/team ` - Team visibility and collaboration context.
- ` /portal/announcements ` - Internal operational updates and notices.
- ` /portal/profile ` - Personal profile and account details.
- ` /portal/earnings ` - Commission visibility for employee or intern roles.

## Standard Employee Workflow for Closing Deals

### 1) Start in the dashboard
Open ` /portal ` at the start of your shift.

Check active tasks, pending follow-ups, and any blockers that can affect deal closure.

### 2) Work each active client case
Use ` /portal/case-management ` as your primary execution area.

For every active deal-related case, confirm:
- Client communication is up to date.
- Delivery requirements are clear.
- Promised outputs are completed or scheduled.
- Next action and owner are clearly identified.

### 3) Follow approved playbooks only
Use ` /portal/knowledge-base ` before contacting a client or marking a case as closure-ready.

Always use the approved:
- Discovery and closing scripts.
- Objection handling guidance.
- Handover and escalation SOPs.
- Documentation standards.

### 4) Use official templates and assets
Use ` /portal/resources ` for approved documents and communication assets.

Do not use outdated local files or informal templates for client-facing communication.

### 5) Coordinate with your team
Use ` /portal/team ` and ` /portal/announcements ` to stay aligned on:
- Policy or process changes.
- Priority shifts.
- Handover expectations.
- Deadline-sensitive updates.

### 6) Mark case as closure-ready and escalate
When a deal is ready for final commercial closure, ensure the case record is complete and escalate for admin action.

Employee responsibility:
- Ensure all case notes are complete and clear.
- Confirm client intent and readiness are documented.
- Attach required supporting context for admin handoff.

Admin responsibility (outside employee route):
- Final status updates.
- Quotation and contract finalization.
- Invoice issuance and payment status controls.
- Final commercial closure state.

### 7) Track commission visibility
Use ` /portal/earnings ` to monitor your own commission-related visibility after successful closures.

If values seem incorrect, escalate with the case reference and summary.

## Employee Closing Checklist

Use this checklist before escalating a deal for final closure:

- [ ] Client needs and scope are clearly documented in case records.
- [ ] Latest client interactions are logged.
- [ ] Delivery progress and pending actions are visible.
- [ ] Required SOPs/scripts were followed from the knowledge base.
- [ ] Correct templates/resources were used.
- [ ] Case is marked closure-ready with complete handoff notes.
- [ ] Escalation includes all context needed by admin.
- [ ] Earnings page reviewed after closure for visibility.

## Common Errors to Avoid

- Marking a case as closure-ready with incomplete notes.
- Using unapproved scripts or outdated templates.
- Failing to capture client confirmation details.
- Escalating without a clear next action or owner.
- Assuming commercial closure is complete before admin confirms it.

## Escalation Standard (Employee to Admin)

When escalating a closure-ready case, include:
- Case reference.
- Client name and current status summary.
- What is complete.
- What remains (if anything).
- Exact admin action requested.

Keep escalation messages concise, factual, and traceable to case records.
